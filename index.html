The Black Friday Arcade

A full Black Friday and Cyber Monday email campaign built in Klaviyo and styled like a retro arcade. It runs from VIP early access and subscriber-first drops through Black Friday, Shop Small Saturday and the final hours, with every send mapped on one campaign calendar.
